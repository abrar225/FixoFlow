Hands-free

Press once to start. Press again to finish.

Click a shortcut, then press and release your choice. Escape cancels. Changes save automatically after a short pause.

Fn/Globe, standalone modifiers, letters, and exposed mouse buttons are supported. Caps Lock can toggle hands-free, but cannot act as a held key. System-reserved keys may be unavailable. Primary mouse clicks trigger dictation only outside this window.

Dictation mode

Choose how local formatting handles your transcript.

Language & recognition
English is the default. Other languages require the optional Base multilingual pack. Multilingual support is experimental. Accuracy varies; Hindi testing has shown substantial errors.

US, UK and India English use the same recognition model. Regional spelling is not forced; no English spelling instructions are sent to the recognizer. Chinese prefers Simplified characters; Hindi prefers Devanagari. Mixed-language accuracy requires testing.

Base multilingual pack · not installed

Off preserves literal words such as period, colon and camel case. English commands only. Verbatim bypasses formatting and dictionary corrections.
Speech model
Fewer threads leave more CPU for other apps. Transcription may take longer; the speech model stays the same.
Base English is bundled. Other models must already be available locally.
